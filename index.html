Our Story

Small Batches, Serious Chocolate

Meltora began with one simple obsession: a brownie that melts the moment it touches your tongue. Every batch is still baked by hand in small quantities, using real chocolate, real butter, roasted hazelnuts and generous fillings — never shortcuts, never mass production.

Each brownie weighs 75 g and is baked fresh for your pre-order, then packed in a kraft gift box. Four fillings — Choco Melt, Double Choco Melt, Peanut Melt and Nutella Melt — and one idea behind all of them: made to melt.
